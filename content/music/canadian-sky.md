# Canadian Sky

**The atmosphere of transformation.**

Canadian Sky is the musical project where Gerry Morgan's 
songwriting meets its most collaborative and atmospheric 
expression. 

It is more than a band; it is a space for the "Prairie-Bhakti" 
fusion that defines Gerry's modern work—a sound that marries 
the liturgical darkness of Western folk with the ancient, 
meditative pull of Indian classical instrumentation.

---

## The Vision

Under the flagship name **Canadian Sky**, Gerry continues to 
explore the persistent question of his career: *What does 
transformation cost?*

From the soaring heights of the aurora borealis to the 
grounded grit of Calgary's prairie soil, Canadian Sky is 
the soundtrack for the journey between who we were and 
who we are becoming.
