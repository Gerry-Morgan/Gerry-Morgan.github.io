# Imperfect Inspiration

**A new collection of 10 tracks, releasing June 8th, 2026.**

This album marks a creatively concentrated period for Gerry Morgan, 
released on his 73rd birthday. It is a celebration of character 
found in flaws, the mystery of the journey, and the beauty of 
imperfection itself.

## Featured Tracks

**Imperfection is Where I Want to Be**
The title track and the heart of the album. A song about stopping 
the search for "perfect" and finding the gold in the cracks.

**In Your Flaw You Find Your Character**
A driving, rhythmic exploration of the idea that we are defined 
by where we've been broken and how we've healed.

**Don't Lose the Mystery**
A folk-rock anthem about the importance of the questions that 
don't have easy answers.
