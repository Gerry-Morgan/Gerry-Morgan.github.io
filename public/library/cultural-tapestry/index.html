Cultural Tapestry: Identity and Its Role in Canada's National Cohesion

Society & Culture

by Gerry Morgan

📥 Download PDF ☕ Donate via Buy Me a Coffee

Cultural Tapestry is a thoughtful, research-grounded exploration of Canadian identity. Gerry Morgan investigates how language, heritage, art, and shared values weave together to create a sense of national belonging — even in a country defined by its diversity.

The book challenges simplistic narratives about unity and difference, instead offering a nuanced portrait of a nation always in the process of becoming.

For anyone who has ever asked: What does it mean to be Canadian?
